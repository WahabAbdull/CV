Methodology

A systematic, repeatable approach to problem-solving and product development.

01

Identification

Literature review and market research to define constraints, establish baseline performance metrics, and validate core assumptions before CAD initiation.

02

Conceptualization

Parametric 3D CAD modeling in SolidWorks focusing on top-down assembly, robust sketch relations, and geometric tolerances.

03

Analysis

Iterative Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to validate structural integrity, thermal loads, and fluid behavior.

04

Prototyping

Optimization of parts for Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing), CNC Machining, or Injection Molding (DFMA).

05

Documentation

Generation of precise 2D technical drawings with GD&T and comprehensive Bill of Materials (BOM) for production handover.
